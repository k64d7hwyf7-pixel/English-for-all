1

Listen

Hear a phrase spoken clearly, with the Mongolian right beside it so the meaning is never a guess.

2

Repeat it, record it, try it again. Small sounds add up to big confidence.

3

Take it to a café, a classroom or a job interview, then come back and tell us how it went.

For parents

Everything you need to follow your child's English, and a few easy ways to help at home.

What your child practises

Everyday phrases for greetings, cafés and getting around, plus short drills for sounds such as th, w and v. Every phrase has its Mongolian meaning beside it.

Help at home

  • Ask your child to teach you one new phrase each day.
  • Watch a short video lesson together and repeat it slowly.
  • Praise the effort. Clear speaking matters more than perfect sounds.
